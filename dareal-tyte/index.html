What DAREALTYTE actually does

Two problems, one pipeline: pulling a site out of the archive, and getting it back online without the mess that usually comes with it.

Full historical reconstruction

Retrieves the archived HTML, CSS, images, and scripts for a domain at a given point in time — not just a single page, but the layout and asset references that made the original site work.

Automatic cleanup

Strips the Wayback Machine's own injected toolbar, rewrites archived asset URLs back to their original form, and neutralizes a blocklist of known spam link patterns left behind by old link injections.

One-click production deployment

The cleaned result ships straight to a live, production Vercel deployment — no manual file wrangling, no separate hosting setup.

Custom domain mapping

Attach your own domain to the freshly deployed project and verify its DNS configuration directly from the same pipeline.
