End to end

What happens to a dollar.

Four steps, in order, because the money genuinely moves through them in this sequence.

01

Advertiser pays

They set a rate and a budget. Stripe takes the full amount once; budget divided by rate is how many waits they own.

02

A human reads it

Paid campaigns wait in a queue. Approved ones start rotating; rejected ones never reach anyone's app.

03

A model starts thinking

Your app calls the unit. One campaign is drawn, weighted by its rate, and exactly one view leaves its balance.

04

You're credited

40% of that rate lands in your balance on the same database write. Not a nightly job that might not run.
