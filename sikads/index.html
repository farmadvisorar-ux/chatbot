The rate is the advertiser's call

They pick anything from $1.00 to $100.00 per 1,000 views. Pay more and you rotate more often — the board sorts itself.

Views are spent, not estimated

A campaign's balance drops by exactly one on each view served, and stops the moment it hits zero. No overdelivery, no rounding in our favour.

Every ad is read before it runs

Paid doesn't mean live. A human checks each campaign, so what lands on your site isn't a scam link that bought its way through.

End to end

What happens to a dollar.

Four steps, in order, because the money genuinely moves through them in this sequence.

01

Advertiser pays

They set a rate and a budget. Stripe takes the full amount once; the budget divided by the rate is how many views they own.

02

A human reads it

Paid campaigns wait in a queue. Approved ones start rotating; rejected ones never appear on anyone's site.

03

The board picks

Each load draws one campaign, weighted by its rate, and spends exactly one view from its balance.

04

You're credited

40% of that campaign's rate lands in your balance on the same write. Not a nightly job that might not run.
